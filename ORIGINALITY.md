# Originality Declaration (SIH-2026, §8)

All substantive code in this repository was developed during the hackathon
window (10-15 September 2026) for this problem statement. No public
real-vs-fake notebook was copied.

## Third-party components used (allowed & cited per §8)
- Libraries: Flask, NumPy, OpenCV, scikit-learn, joblib, Matplotlib, Pillow,
  onnxruntime, PyTorch + torchvision (training only, on Google Colab).
- Pretrained backbone: EfficientNet-B0 ImageNet weights (torchvision), used
  for transfer learning as encouraged by §3.1 and §6.
- Datasets (all public, cited in README): CIFAKE (provided-style core set),
  BSDS500 real photos, official sample images from the Stable Diffusion,
  latent-diffusion, DiT, SDXL (Stability generative-models) and FLUX
  (black-forest-labs) repositories.
- AI coding assistants were used during development, as explicitly permitted
  by §8 ("AI coding assistants may be used; the working system and its
  evaluation are what get scored").

No images of real, identifiable people were scraped or added by the team.
